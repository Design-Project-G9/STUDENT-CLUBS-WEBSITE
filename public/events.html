Host: Student Sport Club

Campus Sports & Fitness Showcase

Target Audience: All university students, faculty, and sports enthusiasts (Open to all skill levels)

Thursday, October 15, 2026

4:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Main Gymnasium & Outdoor Turf Field

Event Schedule & Activities

Time Slot Activity Details
4:00 PM – 4:15 PM Check-in & Welcome Welcome remarks, distribution of wristbands, event maps, and free water bottles.
4:15 PM – 4:30 PM Group Warm-up Session Dynamic stretching and light cardio led by student fitness trainers.
4:30 PM – 5:30 PM Mini-Tournaments & Stations

Rotating activities across different areas of the gym and field:

  • Station 1: Volleyball 3v3 Blitz – Fast-paced 10-minute mini matches (Court 1)
  • Station 2: Basketball 3-Point Contest – Timed shooting challenge with prizes (Court 2)
  • Station 3: Outdoor Turf Drills – Agility ladder, relay races, and casual soccer skills
5:30 PM – 5:50 PM Exhibition Match Feature match between the Sport Club leadership team and varsity athletes.
5:50 PM – 6:00 PM Raffle & Closing Remarks Awarding gift cards, club merchandise, and sign-ups for semester leagues.

Key Highlights & Features

  • Equipment Provided: All balls, nets, and training equipment will be supplied. Participants just need standard athletic wear and indoor court shoes.
  • Refreshments: Hydration stations with water and electrolyte drinks, plus light healthy snacks (granola bars and fresh fruit).
  • Club Recruitment: Opportunity to register for upcoming intramural sports leagues (Volleyball, Basketball, Soccer, and Badminton).

Host: Culinary & Culinary Arts Club

Culinary Masterclass & Taste Workshop

Target Audience: Food lovers, home cooks, and students wanting to learn essential kitchen skills (Beginner friendly)

Sunday, October 18, 2026

2:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Culinary Lab 201 (Student Union)

Event Schedule & Activities

Time Slot Activity Details
2:00 PM – 2:20 PM Registration & Safety Briefing Apron assignment, kitchen station walkthrough, and safety guidelines.
2:20 PM – 3:15 PM Chef's Knife Skills Demo Live demonstration on proper chopping, slicing, and preparing fresh ingredients.
3:15 PM – 4:30 PM Hands-on Cooking Workshop

Prepare 3 quick, budget-friendly gourmet dishes in small groups:

  • Dish 1: Fresh Handmade Pasta with Basil Pesto
  • Dish 2: Pan-Seared Seasoned Vegetables
  • Dish 3: Mini Fruit Parfait Par excellence
4:30 PM – 5:00 PM Plating, Tasting & Social Enjoy your creations together, exchange recipe cards, and vote for best presentation.

Key Highlights & Features

  • Ingredients Provided: All ingredients, aprons, cookware, and cutting boards will be provided.
  • Dietary Accommodations: Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available at all stations.
  • Take-Home Recipe Pack: Print recipe booklet with budget-friendly meal prep tips for students.
